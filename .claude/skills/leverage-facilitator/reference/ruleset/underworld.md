# The underworld

## Ventures

- **Escort Service** — $150, 4 rounds, +2 Heat. Pays 60% of rent CHARGED on your deeds, as dirty cash.
- **Numbers Racket** — $150, 6 rounds, +2 Heat. Pays $60 dirty per round.
- **Chop Shop** — $250, 4 rounds, +3 Heat. Pays $150 dirty per landing on your deeds.

**Speakeasy** — $250, +2 Heat, one 2d6 roll. Payouts by total: 2:$0, 3:$100, 4:$100, 5:$100, 6:$250, 7:$250, 8:$250, 9:$500, 10:$500, 11:$500, 12:$1,200

## Dirty cash

Dirty cash scores $0 at the end. It is worth
something only once laundered.

    haircut = 25% base
            + 5% per Heat point above 3
            capped at 60%

**Ventures cost CLEAN cash and pay DIRTY cash.** At the base haircut alone a venture
must return over 133% of its cost to break even. Read the laundered number, never the
dirty one — simulation puts the gap between correct and naive play at about $1,290.

## Heat and audits

Audits begin in round 13. Each Settlement, every player with
Heat rolls 2d6; a total at or below their Heat is an audit. An audit seizes all dirty
cash and fines $100 per Heat point, payable in clean cash,
with any shortfall capitalising onto the credit line.

Heat decays 1 per round in which the player takes no deliberate dirty action.

## Bribery and insider trading

Bribery costs $200 and +1 Heat, once per round:
force a reroll, cancel a card, or delay a margin call by a round.

Insider trading costs $100 and +1 Heat:
see the top card of the current era deck.
