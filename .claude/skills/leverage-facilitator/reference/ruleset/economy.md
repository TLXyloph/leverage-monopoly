# Economy

- Starting cash: $2,500, a single unified budget
- GO salary: $350, paid from the Treasury
- Carrying cost: $8 per unmortgaged deed, every Settlement, from round 1
- Income tax $200, luxury tax $100, jail fee $50
- Era II stimulus: $300 advanced at the start of round 7 as an interest-bearing LOAN, not a grant
- Houses cost 90% of the printed board figure
- Buildings sell back at 50% of the price paid
- Mortgage raises 50% of face; unmortgaging costs 55.0%
- Physical supply: 32 houses, 12 hotels. Hoarding is legitimate.

All money is integer dollars. Every percentage floors.
